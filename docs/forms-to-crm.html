Fan forms to CRM

Three authenticated routes move form submissions from Tidemail straight into Relay CRM. None of them pass through the warehouse.

Business useTidemail actionRouteRelay CRM result
Harbor FCMatch-day leads → Ticket interest or RSVP form → harbor_leads → Contact, lead and rep alert Summit HCSeason memberships → Membership interest form → summit_memberships → Contact and one open 2027 membership opportunity Both clubsUnsubscribe → Email address and club → crm_unsubscribe → That club's opt-out field

1. Harbor FC match-day leads

Route
harbor_leads
Behaviour
Match or create the Contact. Keep the owner if a rep worked the Contact in the last 90 days, otherwise assign the next eligible rep. Create the mapped lead and alert that rep.
Proof
  1. form row
  2. route receipt
  3. Contact
  4. lead
  5. owner and alert

Replaying the same form row cannot create a second lead. Newsletter-only sign-ups stop before any CRM write.

2. Summit HC season memberships

Route
summit_memberships
Behaviour
Create one open 2027 membership opportunity per Contact. Later submissions update that opportunity without changing its owner. A new one is allowed only after earlier ones are closed.
Proof
  1. form row
  2. stored receipt
  3. Contact
  4. 2027 opportunity

3. Unsubscribe

Route
crm_unsubscribe
Behaviour
Match the Contact by email and set only the named club's do-not-email field. The other club's field is never touched.
Proof
  1. preference row
  2. matched Contact
  3. that club's field
  4. other fields unchanged

An unknown club name or a missing Contact produces no CRM write.

What a success response proves. A 200 from a route proves that route finished. It does not prove the form row existed or that the CRM record is right. Check both ends of the proof chain.

Authentication and retries

All three routes require a function key held by Tidemail. A request without it is rejected before any lookup.

Tidemail retries a failed call three times over ten minutes. Each route stores a receipt keyed on the form row id, so a retry after a timeout finds the receipt and returns the original result instead of writing twice.

How to check one submission end to end
  1. Find the form row in Tidemail by email and submission time.
  2. Find its receipt in the route log by form row id. No receipt means the call never arrived.
  3. Open the Contact in Relay CRM and confirm the lead, opportunity or opt-out field the route should have written.

Duplicate renewal emails, March 4

1,842 Summit HC members got the renewal email twice because the audience job read a day-old CRM copy. Pausing the follow-up and rebuilding the audience was the hotfix; making the job wait for the CRM copy is the durable fix; rotating an expiring key was unrelated cleanup.

  1. Aug 2025
    Audience job built

    Runs at 02:00 and reads the CRM copy that lands at 01:30. One row per Contact per club.

  2. Jan 12
    CRM copy moved to 02:30

    Moved to avoid the vendor's maintenance window. The audience job kept its 02:00 start, so from now on it reads the previous night's copy.

  3. Feb 20
    Email template changed

    Our last change before the incident. It touched the template only, not the audience.

  4. Mar 3 18:00
    Membership team merges 1,842 duplicate Contacts

    Members who renewed in both the app and the box office. A normal, supported action.

  5. Mar 4 02:00
    Audience reads the pre-merge copy

    Each merged member still appears as two Contacts, so as two rows.

  6. Mar 4 09:00
    Renewal email sends twice to 1,842 members

    Reported at 10:15 with two forwarded examples.

  7. Mar 4 11:30
    Follow-up paused, audience rebuilt

    Rebuilt from the 02:30 copy: 1,842 fewer rows, no duplicate emails.

Verified Inferred Reported symptom

Hotfix

Pause the Mar 5 follow-up and rebuild the audience from the fresh copy.

Durable fix

Start the audience job when the CRM copy finishes, not at a clock time.

Unrelated cleanup

Rotate the email-platform key found expiring during the review.

Trigger and cause

Trigger
The March 3 merge. Merging Contacts is supported and correct; it is not the cause and does not get special handling.
Cause
Since January 12 the audience job has started before the CRM copy it depends on. Any change made in CRM during the day reaches emails one day late.
Contained by
Nothing. The email platform does not deduplicate across Contacts, so both rows sent.
Regression test
A fixture with two Contacts merged after the last copy. The audience must contain the member once, and the job must refuse to start on a copy older than six hours.

What we did not change, and why

We did not switch deduplication to email address. Family members often share one address and each needs their own renewal. The durable fix removes the stale read instead.
